CHICAGO, Feb. 20, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — LanzaTech Global, Inc. (NASDAQ: LNZA) (“LanzaTech” or the “Company”), a carbon management company providing a differentiated syngas-to-ethanol solution, today announced that it will issue its fourth quarter and full-year 2024 financial results before financial markets in the United States open on Monday, March 17, 2025.
